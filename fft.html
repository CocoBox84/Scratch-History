After 45th East Sovia war (of the internet) (1997-1998), the Internet Board of Sovia, Internet board of the world, and the Dolly Internet Alliance declared that the refugees of the "Turtlepaint Project" (later the "Scratch Project") would become it's own nation under the seal of 'The Internet'. It officially became the "Turtlepaint Republic" on 4/7/1999. On 8/3/2002, the "Turtlepaint Republic" was changed to the "Scratch Republic" after the MIT military conquered the area. It was not simply a name change, it changed the Republic's government completely after the restart of the "TurtlePaint project" to MIT's "Scratch Project".

Body's of the republic

The republic itself, is top head of anything that has to do with Scratch. It controls the inner government, as well as the outer government. The inner is replaceable, And can be switched and swapped, it also has no limit's other then the rules of the republic itself. The first inner government was the "Scratch Republic of West Sovia" (2002 - 2003). When an inner government collapses, it can be replaced with a better one. Preventing the risk of the collapse of the full republic.

Scratch Republic of West Sovia.

Flag of the Scratch Republic of West Sovia.

The "Scratch Republic of West Sovia" was the first nation to take the role of "Inner government of the Scratch Republic". When Sovia collapsed and split into four in 2000, West Sovia promised the "Scratch Republic" protection from it's enemies if it refused to rebel. So the "Scratch Republic of West Sovia", was born. But in 2003 after the collapse of West Sovia, The Scratch Republic of West Sovia did to. And the "Scratch Empire" took it's spot. In 2002 development of Scratch would start.

Scratch Empire (2003-2004)

The new inner government, the "Scratch Empire" did not rely on foreign powers. But it was weak from the start. It was also temporary, collapsing after President Jack Houlms overthrew it, and built the "State of Scratch", which still stands today.
